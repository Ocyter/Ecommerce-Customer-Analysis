CASE STUDY 03 · GEOGRAPHIC PERFORMANCE

How Metric Choice Changes Location Rankings

Which locations rank highest, and does the answer change with the performance measure?

8 LOCATIONS500 CUSTOMERS2 PERFORMANCE METRICSCUSTOMER DATASET

THE FINDING

81%

of Pune customers purchased at least once, but the city averaged only 2.14 purchases per customer.

Ahmedabad had a 77% purchase rate and 2.45 average purchases per customer.

METHOD & EVIDENCE

Two definitions, with the same denominator.

  1. Group by location. Count customer records and group the purchase indicator by location.
  2. Calculate both measures. Purchase rate counts customers with at least one purchase. Average purchase count includes everyone in the location.
  3. Compare rankings. Sort the location summary separately by each measure. Compare Pune and Ahmedabad to see where the order changes.
  4. Avoid an investment claim. Sample sizes range from 56 to 71 customers per location. Percentages are rounded, with no uncertainty estimates, revenue or marketing-cost evidence.

Data context

An audit reproduced the buyer counts, cart cross-tab and location purchase rankings on a public comparison file. Some browsing, page and cart averages differ from the original report. The historical file, its origin and reuse terms remain unverified. See the source comparison and discrepancies.

Interpret these as descriptive portfolio findings, not audited company results or an improvement delivered for a client.

THE BUSINESS QUESTION

Can one KPI tell us which market is best?

No. Purchase rate measures the share of customers who bought at least once. Average purchases measures purchase depth across the location. They answer different questions.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Rank markets by the decision.

01

Investigate why Delhi and Kolkata rank highly in this sample before proposing an investment decision.

02

Investigate repeat-purchase behaviour in markets with strong conversion but lower purchase depth.

03

Use multiple KPIs instead of ranking locations on purchase rate alone.

04

Add revenue, order value, acquisition source and repeat timing before making investment decisions.
