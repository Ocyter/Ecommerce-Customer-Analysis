OPERATIONS ANALYTICS / CAPITAL BIKESHARE

When should a bike-share operator check capacity?

Working-day rental demand peaked at 17:00, averaging 525 rides per observed hour. On weekends and holidays the peak shifted four hours earlier, to 13:00 (373 rides).

Washington, DC area · Jan 2011 to Dec 2012 · 3,292,679 recorded rentals

Decision: Review availability before the clock-hour peaks, using different windows for working days and days off. Station-level evidence is needed before assigning bikes or staff.

One staffing schedule would miss one of the peaks

Working days also show a morning peak: 477 rentals per observed 08:00 hour. At 17:00, registered riders accounted for 89.2% of rentals; at the weekend/holiday peak their share was 63.4%. Registration is recorded; trip purpose is not.

03 / OPERATIONS DECISION

Check stations before moving bikes

For a historical staffing review, examine working-day coverage before 08:00 and 17:00, and weekend or holiday coverage before 13:00. System-wide trips show when bikes were rented, not where demand went unmet.

For a present-day pilot, first obtain recent station-level departures, arrivals, capacity and minutes empty or full. Select comparable stations and track empty/full minutes per service hour alongside rebalancing cost. Those measures would test whether a schedule change actually helped.
