WHAT I FOUND

Browsing metrics tell only part of the story.

Mobile was the largest group.

Mobile accounted for 178 customers and averaged 2.53 purchases per customer. The differences across devices were modest.

Delhi led on both measures.

It had the highest purchase rate (87%) and average purchase count (2.73) in the sample.

Browsing and cart signals were weak.

Browsing time, pages viewed and cart items all had very weak linear relationships with the number of purchases.

WHAT I’D INVESTIGATE NEXT

Where do customers stop?

I’d collect timestamps for product views, cart additions, checkout steps and purchases, then compare where sessions end across devices. That would show which stages deserve a closer look.
