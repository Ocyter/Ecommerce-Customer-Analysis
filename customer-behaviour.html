KEY FINDINGS

What the analysis
actually says.

01

80.2% purchased

401 of 500 customers made at least one purchase, leaving 99 customers with zero purchases.

02

Mobile leads

Mobile is the largest device segment at 178 customers and has the highest average purchases at 2.53.

03

Delhi leads

Delhi has the highest purchase rate at 87% and the highest average purchase count at 2.73.

04

Correlations are weak

Purchases have very weak linear relationships with browsing time, pages viewed, cart items and age.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Turn the signal
into a next move.

01

Prioritise mobile UX because mobile is the largest customer segment and slightly leads device-level purchase depth.

02

Investigate the 99 zero-purchase customers as a natural segment for checkout-friction and remarketing analysis.

03

Compare locations by the metric that matters: Delhi and Kolkata lead purchase reach, while other markets reveal different purchase-depth patterns.

04

Collect event-level timestamps, product IDs, prices, traffic source and checkout steps before making stronger funnel or predictive claims.
