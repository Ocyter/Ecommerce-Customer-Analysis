Why I chose analytics

I’ve always been curious about what sits behind the numbers. I want to understand why something happened, whether the information is reliable, and what we can learn from it. Analytics gives me a practical way to explore those questions.

During my internship at Genesys, I worked on cleaning data, building dashboards and supporting reporting for product and project teams. I enjoyed taking information that was difficult to work with and making it clearer and more useful.

My work in data quality and governance also taught me that a good-looking dashboard is only valuable when you can trust what’s behind it. Today, I bring that same approach to my own work and to mentoring other analysts.

I hold a BSc in Computer Science from Enugu State University of Science and Technology.
